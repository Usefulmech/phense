Data Folder Architecture & Sourcing Pillars

To prevent black-box opacity, the project implements a 3-tier data lifecycle architecture (data/raw, data/reference, and data/processed):

storage data/raw/

External downloaded source files, NiMet climate records, and NIHSA flood bulletin archives.

map data/reference/

Spatial GIS vector reference databases: nigeria_rivers.json (12 major river channels) & nigeria_lgas.json (774 LGA centroids).

table_chart data/processed/

Cleaned training dataset (nigeria_flood_training.csv) generated by anchoring real LGA coordinates to satellite elevation and climate baselines.
