Visual Soil Identification Guide

Tap the soil type that best matches your local ground conditions:

Clay Soil (Cracked / Heavy Blocks) High Water Retention

Heavy, sticky wet texture with dense cohesive clods. Develops deep shrinkage cracks when dry. Water puddles and drains very slowly.

Sandy Soil (Coarse Grains) Fast Drainage

Coarse & gritty individual sand particles. Light golden-brown color. Water soaks in almost instantly with minimal surface runoff.

Loamy Soil (Crumbly Organic Humus) Balanced

Rich dark brown organic earth with soft, spongy crumb structure. Retains adequate moisture without remaining waterlogged.

Silty Soil (Fine Micro-particles / Mud) Saturates Quickly

Smooth, flour-like micro-particles when dry; turns into slippery mud when wet. Highly prevalent near riverbanks and floodplains.
